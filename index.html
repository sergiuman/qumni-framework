About QUMNI

Why this exists

The world is drowning in self-help content with no scientific foundation, and buried under academic research that never reaches the people who need it. QUMNI bridges the gap.

Every framework published here meets a simple standard: Is it grounded in established science? Does it explain something real? Can a person use it to make a better decision today?

The two frameworks work as a system. PCF (Perceptual Control Framework) addresses the quality of your perception — what you notice, how you interpret it, and whether your mental model of reality is calibrated enough to act on. WSO (Will, Skill, Opportunity) addresses the quality of your action — diagnosing which of the three pillars is missing and prescribing the specific intervention. See clearly, then act precisely.

QUMNI is not a guru. It's a toolkit. Use what works. Challenge what doesn't. Build on what's here.
